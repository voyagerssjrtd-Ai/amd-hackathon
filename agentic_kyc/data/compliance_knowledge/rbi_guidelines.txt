Regulated entities should adopt a risk-based approach to customer due diligence.

Customer identification must rely on reliable and independent source documents.

Enhanced Due Diligence is expected for higher-risk customer categories.

Risk classification should consider customer profile, geography, and transactional behavior.

Ongoing monitoring is required for customers classified as high risk.

Politically exposed persons require additional scrutiny during onboarding.

Suspicious indicators identified during onboarding should be escalated for investigation.

Institutions should maintain adequate records supporting KYC decisions.

Automated decision systems should provide explainable outcomes.

Human oversight should remain available for complex or high-risk cases.
