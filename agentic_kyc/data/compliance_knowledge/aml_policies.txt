Politically Exposed Persons (PEPs) require Enhanced Due Diligence.

Enhanced Due Diligence should include source-of-funds verification and senior management approval.

Document tampering indicators should result in escalation for manual review.

Repeated inconsistencies between PAN and Aadhaar information should increase risk classification.

Adverse media findings should trigger enhanced monitoring.

Customers identified on internal blacklists must be escalated immediately.

Missing critical KYC documents should prevent automatic approval.

Synthetic identity indicators require escalation to fraud investigation teams.

Financial profiles inconsistent with declared occupation should be reviewed.

All KYC decisions must remain explainable and auditable.
